About

Respectful greetings, and welcome

I am Abbas Shemirani, with about 7 years of experience in ideation, hand design and design with various software such as ZBrush, Matrix and Blender — and I am with you from design to making.

I have worked in several well-known and lesser-known workshops, I have more than 1,000 designs and finished pieces, and besides Iran I have designed for three different countries. All of the designs are my own.

Drawing on my father’s experience in gold and jewellery making and stone-setting, and on my own design background, every piece is sold in limited numbers with a manufacturing guarantee, and partnership-in-production agreements are also accepted.

For prices, collaboration and buying our pieces, please send us a message through the Contact section.
