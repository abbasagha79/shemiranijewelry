About

I'm Abbas Shemirani

For more than seven years my work has been imagining and designing jewellery — by hand and on the computer — and carrying each design all the way through to the finished piece in gold. I've made work for clients in Iran and three other countries, and every design on this site, from the first idea to the last detail, is my own.

Behind every piece are my father's thirty-five years at the bench — making and stone-setting. What I learned from him is what lets an idea in my head come out real: something that can genuinely be made, and made precisely.

I hold two national design certificates and two international ones, and I earned my entrepreneurship certificate at sixteen. Every design — and the pieces made from it — is released in a limited edition, so each one stays rare.

I work with several workshops remotely. I'm based in Karaj, where we also keep two gold boutiques.
